The Beijing robot half marathon is an event held in Beijing E-Town, where humanoid robots run a 21 km half marathon alongside human runners on separate lanes.

The race is less about sport and more about testing robotics in public conditions. It measures balance, battery life, obstacle avoidance, speed control, durability, and how well robots handle uneven ground, slopes, and long-distance movement.

These are the exact problems companies need to solve to deploy robots in warehouses, factories, rescue work, and public services. A 21 km race is just the cheapest way to stress-test all of them at once.
